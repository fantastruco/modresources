## _Because Trainer battles are better with a friend~!_

![Description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fantastruco/modresources/refs/heads/main/deploy/web/common/DescriptionV1.png)

Cobble Co-op turns Radical Cobblemon Trainers battles into a proper two-player
challenge!

Instead of one Player starting the battle while everybody else watches, you can
invite a friend, choose both teams and fight the opposing Trainer together.

The mod includes its own setup menus, shared team rules, Trainer randomizers,
different AI difficulties and an optional Reward Bag system. Most battle changes
only last during the current battle, so both Players continue using their normal
Pokémon after it ends.

&nbsp;

![Features](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fantastruco/modresources/refs/heads/main/deploy/web/common/FeaturesV1.png)

### Two different Co-op modes!

- **One Trainer (2 vs 1):** Both Players fight one Trainer. That Trainer supplies
  the complete opposing team.
- **Two Trainers (2 vs 2):** Both Players fight two compatible Trainers found
  nearby. Each Trainer controls their own side of the opposing team.

Every Player chooses their own Pokémon and their battle order before the fight
starts. The server checks both teams together, so shared clauses cannot be
bypassed by selecting at the same time.

### A complete list of Handicaps!

Handicaps can change the team size, levels, HP, PP, moves, IVs, EVs, items and
many other parts of the battle.

There are also clauses for species, shared types, type combinations, special
Pokémon and Generations. Rules that affect the allied team treat both Players as
one complete group.

The Handicap menu includes a search bar, small icon badges and Additional
Information for rules that need examples or a better explanation.

### Trainer Randomizers!

The opposing team can be kept normal, partially randomized or completely
randomized. Depending on the selected options, CobbleCoop can also randomize:

- Trainer Pokémon.
- Moves and move legality.
- Abilities.
- Held items.

Competitive randomization tries to avoid filling the opposing team with weak
pre-evolutions and can prepare more useful teams for double battles.

### Better Trainer AI!

Choose between **Standard**, **Strategic** and **Competitive** AI Difficulty.

Strategic and Competitive Trainers can coordinate their targets, avoid wasting
support moves, protect an ally, switch when needed and make better use of their
complete team. Competitive mode also considers available battle gimmicks such
as Mega Evolution, Terastallization, Dynamax, Gigantamax and Z-Moves.

### Reward Bags!

Winning a valid Co-op battle can give each Player their own Reward Bag. Bags can
be **Common**, **Uncommon**, **Rare**, **Epic** or **Legendary**.

Harder Handicaps improve the chance of receiving a better rarity. Trainer level,
team level, repeated victories and personal pity are also considered.

### Battle Presets and configuration!

Your last accepted setup can be used again, or saved as a named Battle Preset.
If one server does not allow part of a preset, the menu shows which option was
rejected instead of silently changing it.

&nbsp;

![How to Play](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fantastruco/modresources/refs/heads/main/deploy/web/common/HowToPlayV1.png)

1. Find a Radical Cobblemon Trainer.
2. Sneak and right-click the Trainer to open **Co-op Battle Setup**.
3. Choose **One Trainer (2 vs 1)** or **Two Trainers (2 vs 2)**.
4. Choose the team size and any Handicaps you want to use.
5. Select a nearby Player and send the invitation.
6. After they accept, both Players choose their Pokémon and battle order.
7. When both Players are ready, the battle begins!

![Requirements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/fantastruco/modresources/refs/heads/main/deploy/web/common/RequirementsV1.png)

CobbleCoop is made for **Minecraft 1.21.1** and **Java 21**. Use the same
loader and mod versions on the client and server.

### Fabric

- Fabric Loader `0.17.2` or newer.
- Fabric API `0.116.6+1.21.1` or newer.
- Cobblemon `1.7.3+1.21.1`.
- Radical Cobblemon Trainers API `0.15.2-beta`.
- Radical Cobblemon Trainers `0.18.1-beta`.
- Forge Config API Port `21.1.1`.

### NeoForge

- NeoForge `21.1.182` or newer for Minecraft 1.21.1.
- Cobblemon `1.7.3+1.21.1`.
- Radical Cobblemon Trainers API `0.15.2-beta`.
- Radical Cobblemon Trainers `0.18.1-beta`.
- Architectury API `13.0.8`.
- Kotlin for Forge `5.10.0`.
